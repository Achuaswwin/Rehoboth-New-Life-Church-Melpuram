About Rehoboth

Our Mission & Community

Rehoboth New Life Church (Rehoboth New Life Ministries) in Melpuram, Pacode is a spirit-filled church family committed to preaching the uncompromised Word of God.

We focus on strengthening families, encouraging youth, upholding prayer ministry, and reaching the wider Kanyakumari district with the gospel of hope and salvation.

  • ✓ Bible-centered Teaching & Worship
  • ✓ Active Youth, Women's, and Men's Fellowships
  • ✓ Weekly Fasting & Intercessory Prayer Meetings
